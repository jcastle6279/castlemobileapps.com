Chain Reaction Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Castle Mobile Apps ("we," "us") handles information in the mobile game Chain Reaction (the "Game"). For questions or privacy requests, email jcass6279@gmail.com.

1. Information stored by the Game

Chain Reaction is designed to be playable without registering an account or providing your name, email address, or phone number. Game progress, coin balances, booster inventory, and similar gameplay information are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a player-account database or an online leaderboard for the Game.

Local game data may be lost if you delete the Game, clear its data, or change devices without a compatible device backup. Standard device backup features may be controlled by your operating system and its settings.

2. Optional rewarded advertisements

Chain Reaction offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. You can choose whether to watch an advertisement to receive an in-game reward; watching advertisements is not required for ordinary gameplay.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers where available and permitted, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. They may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and—where permitted—advertising personalization. The exact processing depends on your device, region, settings, and consent choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the Game will present privacy choices for advertising through Google's consent management tools. You may be able to accept, decline, or manage certain uses of information depending on your region. Advertising availability may vary based on your choices and technical conditions. The Game is intended to remain playable even when a rewarded advertisement is unavailable.

Where a privacy options form is required, the Game will provide a Privacy and cookie settings entry point to revisit your choices. You can also manage applicable device-level advertising permissions in iOS Settings. For privacy questions or requests, contact jcass6279@gmail.com.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not maintain a separate database of players' personal profiles or sell player account information. Information processed by Google AdMob and its partners is governed by their applicable policies, retention practices, and legal obligations. Locally saved gameplay information remains on your device until removed through the Game, app deletion, or device-management features, subject to device backups.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is not intended to request personal contact details from children. Advertising and age-related settings must be configured appropriately for the Game's intended audience and applicable laws before public release. If you believe information about a child has been handled improperly, contact us at jcass6279@gmail.com.

6. International processing

Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the one in which you live. Their privacy policies describe their handling of international data transfers and related safeguards.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the Game's features, advertising practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
